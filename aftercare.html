After Care Guide

Your beautiful nails deserve a little love between appointments.

✧ ♡ ✧

Hi, beautiful! ♡
Thank you for choosing Naileditby_gel. Here are a few simple tips to help care for your manicure and keep your nails looking lovely.

♡

Moisturize daily

Apply cuticle oil twice a day to hydrate the skin around your nails and keep your manicure looking fresh.

💧

Go easy on water

For the first 24 hours, avoid soaking your hands in water for long periods.

✧

Don't pick or peel

Picking, biting, or peeling gel can damage your natural nail. Please don't use your nails as tools.

♨

Limit excess heat

Avoid prolonged exposure to very hot water or steam, especially during the first 24 hours.

♡

Protect your hands

Wear gloves when washing dishes, gardening, or handling harsh cleaning chemicals.

◷

Plan your next visit

Book your maintenance appointment in around 2–4 weeks, depending on your nail growth and service.
