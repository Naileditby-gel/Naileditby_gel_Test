♡ Booking, Safety & Salon Policies

  • Safety first: For everyone's safety, Gel may decline or reschedule an appointment if you have open wounds, cuts, swelling, infection, significant finger/nail injuries, or if you are feeling unwell.
  • Price disclaimer: Final pricing is confirmed after Gel reviews your nail condition, design complexity, inspiration photos and whether existing enhancements need removal. Prices may vary based on these factors.
  • Removal policy: Additional removal charges may apply, especially for work done by another nail technician. Removal of previous Naileditby_gel work may also incur a fee depending on the product and condition. Gel will confirm any removal fee before you agree to proceed.
  • Custom deposit: If your request is accepted, Gel will provide the total price, required deposit and payment instructions after reviewing your request. The deposit is non-refundable once paid, subject to applicable law and any specific agreement.
  • Payment deadline: A 1-hour payment window begins when Gel sends the payment instructions. If payment details are not submitted before the deadline, the request may expire and the time slot will be released. Submitting payment proof does not confirm the appointment; Gel must verify the payment.
  • Late policy: 20 minutes late = ₱100 late fee. 1 hour late = appointment automatically voided/cancelled and the deposit is forfeited, unless you have informed Gel and Gel has confirmed a revised arrangement.
  • Last-minute cancellation / no-show: The required deposit is non-refundable.
  • No companions: No companion is allowed unless previously discussed with Gel.
  • 3-day warranty: Eligible service issues are covered for 3 days. Please contact Gel as soon as possible if there is a problem.
  • Parking: There is no parking area at the studio.
